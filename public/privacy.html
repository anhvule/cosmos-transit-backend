Mochi Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Mochi (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides personalized daily horoscopes and astrology guidance. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

To generate your readings, the app may send us:

  • A name or display name you provide
  • Birth date and birth time
  • Birth location (latitude and longitude) and timezone
  • Dates you request readings for

We may also receive technical information needed to operate the service, such as IP address, device type, and basic request logs.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Calculate astrological charts and deliver personalized content
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the app and API
  • Respond to support requests you send us
  • Protect against abuse and ensure service reliability

We do not sell your personal information.

Data storage and retention

Birth and location details are used to fulfill your requests. We retain server logs and related operational data only as long as needed for the purposes above, unless a longer period is required by law.

Sharing

We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to run the app. These providers process data only to provide their services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

Children’s privacy

Mochi is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Your choices

You can stop providing birth and location information by not using features that require them, or by deleting the app. To request deletion of information you have sent us, email the contact address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
