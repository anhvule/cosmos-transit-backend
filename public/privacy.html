Mochi Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Mochi (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides personalized daily horoscopes and astrology guidance. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

We do not store your personal information

We do not create user accounts, and we do not store your personal information on our servers. Birth details and location you enter are used only to generate your reading for that request, then discarded. We do not keep a profile or history of your data.

Information used to generate readings

To calculate your horoscope, the app may send the following information to our API for that request only:

  • A name or display name you provide
  • Birth date and birth time
  • Birth location (latitude and longitude) and timezone
  • Dates you request readings for

This information is processed in memory to produce your result and is not saved to a database or other long-term storage.

Information stored on your device

Any preferences or details you save in the app (for example, birth details for convenience) stay on your device. We do not receive or store that on-device data except when you actively request a reading.

Technical and support information

Like most online services, our hosting provider may automatically process basic technical data such as IP address and request timestamps for security and reliability. We do not use this to identify you or build a personal profile.

If you email us for support, we will use the information you include in that message only to respond to you.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your birth or location details with third parties for marketing. Infrastructure providers that host the API may process requests solely to operate the service.

Children’s privacy

Mochi is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

Because we do not store your personal information, there is no account data for us to delete on our servers. You can remove any information saved in the app by clearing app data or deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
